# Yi Cui

*Ph.D. student, Department of Economics, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill*

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. My name is pronounced **/Ts' ui'/**. I study how formal statistical and machine-learning methods can make economic decisions more credible, robust, and useful.

## Research

My work sits at the intersection of economics, statistics, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in:

- causal inference and robust identification;
- machine learning for economic decision-making;
- generative AI and LLM agents;
- algorithmic pricing and recommendation systems.

See my [Research](/research) page for selected work and my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=HLJKLKIAAAAJ&hl=en) for a complete publication record.
